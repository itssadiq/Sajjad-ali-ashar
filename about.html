AUTHOR BIO

I have never been comfortable staying in one lane.

My work has moved between history, business, writing, and digital strategy — not because I was experimenting, but because I was trying to understand how systems actually work. Whether it is a civilization, a market, or a piece of technology, the underlying question has always been the same: what drives change?

History gave me perspective. Business gave me ground reality. Technology showed me where things are heading. Over time, these did not remain separate interests — they became a single way of thinking.

I have built ventures, worked with businesses, and written across different domains, but I don’t define myself by any one title. What I do is simple: I build, I think, and I write — and I try to connect ideas in a way that actually makes sense in the real world.
